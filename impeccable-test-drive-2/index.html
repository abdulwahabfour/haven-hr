What to expect on the call

Your 45-minute consult is a conversation, not a presentation. Here is how the time usually goes.

  • First 10 minutes: your advisor listens to the situation and asks clarifying questions.
  • Next 25 minutes: options, trade-offs and what a fair outcome looks like for everyone involved.
  • Last 10 minutes: agreeing next steps and what will be in your written summary.

Everything you share on the call stays between you and your advisor, and your written summary arrives in your inbox within a day of the call.
